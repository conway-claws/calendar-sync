Conway CLAWS Community Calendar

Conway High School events in one calendar: district dates, athletics, arts, and CLAWS announcements. Rebuilt every morning.

Subscribe once and events appear in the calendar app you already use, and stay current on their own. Take everything, or just the categories you want.

Google Calendar

  1. Open calendar.google.com on a computer.
  2. Next to Other calendars click +, then From URL.
  3. Paste a calendar address from the table above and click Add calendar.

It then shows up in the Google Calendar app on your phone under Settings → your account, where you can toggle it on.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar.
  2. Paste a calendar address from the table above and tap Next, then Save.

Outlook

  1. In Outlook on the web, open the calendar and choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  2. Paste a calendar address, name it, and add.
